Strong shoulders, sharp minds

In many villages, a donkey does the heavy trips so people can do everything else. Here is what makes them special.

ጠንካራ

Strong

Water, firewood, grain, and market goods. A working donkey hauls loads all day over rough ground and steep paths.

ብልህ

Clever

Donkeys stop when something looks unsafe. That is not stubbornness, it is careful thinking, and they remember routes for years.

ታማኝ

Loyal

They bond closely with their people and with other donkeys, and they notice when a friend is missing.

A day in a donkey's life

From the first light to the last, this is how a working donkey spends the day.

  1. 5:30

    Water run

    Load the jerrycans and reach the well before the heat arrives.

  2. 9:00

    Market trip

    Grain, vegetables, and firewood. The road is long, but she knows it by heart.

  3. 1:00

    Shade and rest

    Fresh water, a pile of hay, and a break under the acacia tree.

  4. 6:00

    Home

    Unload, brush down, and a handful of oats before sunset.
